Why choosing is so hard

With a big backlog every game competes with every other one. You scroll for twenty minutes, wonder whether something takes 5 or 50 hours, and end up opening the usual game.

How Ruletazo helps

Paste your Steam profile or sign in with Steam, and the roulette spins through your library. When it stops, you see how long the game is, what reviews say and how many achievements you have left. Not feeling it? Hit Another.

Filters and friends

Never played, under 2 hours, abandoned, genre, co-op or multiplayer. Add up to 5 friends' libraries to roll only games you all own, or open a group room where everyone vetoes games and watches the same spin live.
